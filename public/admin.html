Find real restaurants, email them, track their status

The agent picks real Amsterdam restaurants with a public email address, drafts a personal first email for each, and waits for your approval. Nothing is sent until you approve it. Replies update each restaurant's status automatically, and any surplus they offer goes to the router and the app.

Restaurant finder

Pulls restaurants, cafés and takeaways in Amsterdam from Google Places (when GOOGLE_PLACES_API_KEY is set) or OpenStreetMap. Google is billed per request: a limited run searches the centre first. Only OpenStreetMap has email addresses, so the outreach agent searches there.
