Fair by design

You donate, you don't pay for the food

Restaurants give their surplus for free. Your donation covers the takeaway packaging and helps keep FoodLoop running. Anything extra is up to you.

    For restaurants

    Less waste, zero admin

    No dashboard to learn. We send you a short message; you reply with what's left, just like texting a friend. We handle the listing, the reservations and the pickup codes.

    • One message a day, at most. Reply STOP and we'll never contact you again.
    • Pickup on your terms. You choose the time window; listings expire automatically.
    • Verified pickups. Every reservation has a 6-digit code.
    • Free for you. Users' donations cover packaging and running costs.

    Questions

    Why is there a limit on how much I can reserve?

    So surplus reaches as many people as possible. Each person can reserve a few portions per listing and from a couple of restaurants per day.

    How do I get alerts?

    Join in the app, share your location and choose a radius. When a restaurant nearby posts food, you get an alert straight away.

    What if I can't make it?

    Cancel the reservation in the app so the portions go back to others.

    Is the food safe?

    It's the same food the restaurant serves that day, collected within the window they set. Check the dietary tags, and ask at pickup if you have allergies.
